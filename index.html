What it is

ReflexTap flashes the screen green at a random moment; you tap as fast as you can. It measures the gap between the cue and your tap to the millisecond and scores the round. Tap before the flash and it is a false start — no cheating the clock. A handful of rounds gives you a median, your best, your 90th-percentile consistency, and a grade from Slow to Elite.

Three modes

Quick Test (five rounds), Endurance (twenty rounds back to back, where staying fast is the whole point), and Anticipation — no countdown, no instructions, just watch the dial and tap the instant it flips.

Private by design

Your scores stay on your device. No account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking. Timing uses a high-resolution monotonic clock on your device.
