ReflexTap

How it works

Pick a mode and start. Wait for the screen to flash green, then tap anywhere as fast as you can. Tapping before the flash is a false start. After each round you see your reaction time; at the end you get a median, best, 90th-percentile, and a grade.

The three modes

  • Quick Test — five rounds for a fast read on your reaction time.
  • Endurance — twenty rounds back to back; staying consistent is the point, so the 90th-percentile time is the score that matters.
  • Anticipation — no countdown or instructions; watch the dial and tap the instant it flips green.

Tips for a better score

  • Rest a finger lightly over the screen so only your reaction — not your reach — is measured.
  • Use Endurance to find your honest, repeatable number rather than a lucky single tap.
  • Adjust the pre-cue wait range and rounds in Settings.

Privacy

ReflexTap stores your scores on your device; its only network connection is to validate in-app purchases. See the Privacy Policy.
