Privacy Policy

ReflexTap · Last updated 22 September 2026

The short version

ReflexTap collects nothing about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no tracking. The tests work fully offline; the app's only network connection is to validate in-app purchases through RevenueCat, described below.

What stays on your device

Your test sessions, reaction times, daily bests, and preferences are stored only on your device (and, if you use it, in your own iCloud-backed device backup via the standard system mechanisms). This data is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and the Developer runs no servers.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

Third parties

The App uses RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) to validate in-app purchases. RevenueCat receives the App Store purchase receipt, an anonymous app user ID created on your device, and the basic technical details any network request carries, such as IP address, app version and device model — never your name, email address or payment details. It processes them under its own privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy. The App contains no advertising, attribution or tracking SDKs.

Permissions

ReflexTap requests no permissions. It does not use location, the microphone, the camera, contacts, motion data, or notifications.

Children

ReflexTap is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages. It collects no data from anyone.
