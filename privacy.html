Privacy Policy

ReflexTap · Last updated 3 June 2026

The short version

ReflexTap collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. The app makes no network requests of any kind and works fully offline.

What stays on your device

Your test sessions, reaction times, daily bests, and preferences are stored only on your device (and, if you use it, in your own iCloud-backed device backup via the standard system mechanisms). This data is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, because the app has no servers and no networking code.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, sell, share, or process any personal data.

Permissions

ReflexTap requests no permissions. It does not use location, the microphone, the camera, contacts, motion data, or notifications.

Children

ReflexTap is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages. It collects no data from anyone.
